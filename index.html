Help and answers

Frequently asked questions

What is DigitalDuniya360?

DigitalDuniya360 is an India-focused online platform for community articles, business listings, classified ads, product offers, jobs and digital resources.

How can I publish a blog?

Create an account, prepare an original and useful article, then submit it through the blog submission form. Submissions remain pending until reviewed.

How can I list products online?

Use Post free ad for a classified product listing. Signed-in users can share product offers and coupons on the products and deals page.

Is DigitalDuniya360 free to use?

Browsing public pages and creating an account are free. Some optional offers may link to third-party sellers; their prices and terms are set by those sellers.

How can I submit an article?

Sign in and use the article submission form. Include accurate details and original writing; publication depends on moderation and editorial guidelines.

How does blog commenting work?

Sign in to submit a relevant comment on a published article. Comments are reviewed before they are visible to other readers.

Can businesses advertise on DigitalDuniya360?

Business owners can submit a directory profile for review and may post classified ads. Keep all claims and contact details accurate.

How can I create an account?

Visit Create an account, complete the registration form and follow any email verification steps shown.

Which categories are available?

Current sections include technology, business, education, property, electronics, jobs, digital marketing, shopping, finance and services. The available listing categories vary by section.

How can I contact DigitalDuniya360?

Send a message through the contact form. Do not include passwords or sensitive financial information.
